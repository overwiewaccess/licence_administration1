Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA)

Switzerland's independent, top financial-market regulator.

  • Supervises banks, securities firms, insurance companies, exchanges, collective investment schemes, fund managers, asset managers, trustees, and FinTech providers.
  • Enforces anti-money laundering (AML) laws, grants operating licenses, conducts risk-based supervision, and protects creditors, investors, and policyholders.

Swiss National Bank (SNB) — Central Bank and Monetary Authority

  • Responsible for Switzerland's monetary policy and the stability of the financial system as a whole.
  • Monitors and holds supervisory powers over systemically important financial market infrastructures (such as large payment systems and securities settlement systems).

Federal Department of Finance (FDF)

  • The federal executive department responsible for drafting financial market legislation, tax policy, and currency matters.
  • FINMA is administratively attached to the FDF, though FINMA operates independently in its day-to-day supervisory duties.
